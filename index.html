About CareWatch

Helping people feel closer to care.

We believe health technology should make it easier—not harder—to understand what is happening and know who to turn to. CareWatch is designed to connect personal health insights with clinicians and trusted caregivers, while keeping people in control.

Our approach

People first. Technology in support.

◎

Earlier conversations

Clearer health context can help people decide when it may be useful to contact a professional.

♧

Connected support

Patients can choose to bring clinicians and trusted caregivers into their health journey.

◈

Responsible insights

Technology can surface patterns, but only qualified professionals can provide medical advice and care.

CareWatch is a prototype concept. The sample insights shown here are not generated by a clinically validated AI system and should not be used for diagnosis or treatment.

Help & support

Answers to common questions about using CareWatch.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my smartwatch?

In a full CareWatch product, open Profile & Settings and follow the device-pairing instructions. This website prototype does not connect to a real watch.

What should I do when I receive an alert?

Read the context and suggested next step. Alerts are not diagnoses. If you feel unwell or are concerned, contact a healthcare professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Can CareWatch replace my doctor?

No. CareWatch is intended to support conversations and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Who can see my information?

You should be able to control what information is shared with clinicians and caregivers. This prototype does not store or transmit real health data.

How do I contact support?

For this prototype, please contact the CareWatch project team through the repository. A production service should provide a verified support contact and privacy notice.

Need more help?

For a real CareWatch service, support should be available to assist with account access, devices, and privacy questions.

Is this an emergency?

CareWatch is not an emergency service. Contact your local emergency number if you need urgent help.
